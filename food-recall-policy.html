Food Safety & Compliance

FOOD RECALL & TRACEABILITY

Effective Date: August 2026

Purpose

Wholesome Plates Catering LLC maintains procedures to promptly identify, isolate, trace, communicate, and remove recalled, held, or potentially unsafe food products from our operations and client locations.

This policy applies to food and food products purchased, received, stored, prepared, packaged, transported, or delivered by Wholesome Plates.

Recall Management

Wholesome Plates designates a Primary Food Recall Contact and a Backup Food Recall Contact responsible for coordinating food recall and hold activities.

The designated contacts maintain access to the information necessary to identify affected products, determine whether those products remain in inventory or entered production, identify client locations that may have received affected products, and coordinate appropriate communication and disposition.

Wholesome Plates maintains 24-hour, seven-day accessibility to an authorized company representative for urgent food or USDA holds and recalls. For urgent food safety or recall matters, contact info@wholesomeplatescatering.com.

Food Recall Procedure

Upon notification of an applicable food recall or food-safety hold, Wholesome Plates will:

  1. Identify the affected product. Available information will be reviewed, including product name, manufacturer or supplier, lot or production code, case information, expiration or use-by information, and other identifying information.
  2. Immediately stop use and distribution. Affected or potentially affected product will not be prepared, served, transported, or delivered.
  3. Isolate the product. Product under WPC's control will be removed from usable inventory, segregated where practicable, and identified DO NOT USE – HOLD/RECALL.
  4. Trace the product. WPC will review applicable purchasing, receiving, inventory, production, and delivery records to determine where the affected product was received, stored, used, or delivered.
  5. Identify affected client locations. Delivery and production records will be used to determine which client sites, if any, may have received the product.
  6. Notify affected clients promptly. WPC will provide the client's designated representative with available product-identification information and instructions necessary to locate and isolate the affected product.
  7. Account for affected inventory. WPC will determine, to the extent reasonably possible, quantities remaining in inventory, used in production, delivered, returned, destroyed, or otherwise disposed of.
  8. Follow disposition instructions. Product will be returned, destroyed, or otherwise handled in accordance with instructions from the manufacturer, distributor, USDA, FDA, Georgia Department of Education, local health authorities, or other applicable authority.
  9. Document the incident. Applicable recall notices, communications, traceability findings, inventory information, corrective actions, disposition, and replacement records will be maintained.
  10. Maintain meal-service continuity. When appropriate, WPC will coordinate safe replacement products to minimize interruption to client meal service.

Product Traceability

Wholesome Plates maintains records designed to trace food from receipt through production and delivery.

Depending upon the product and operation, records may include:

  • supplier invoices and purchase records;
  • receiving documentation;
  • manufacturer and product information;
  • lot, case, production, expiration, or use-by information when available;
  • inventory records;
  • production records;
  • menu information;
  • delivery manifests and receipts;
  • client/site delivery information; and
  • recall, hold, corrective-action, and disposition records.

Food delivered by suppliers becomes WPC-controlled inventory upon receipt and acceptance.

Wholesome Plates maintains designated secured storage for company inventory within its shared commercial kitchen environment. Refrigerated and frozen inventory is maintained within designated locked cages in shared temperature-controlled storage areas.

Annual Mock Recall

Wholesome Plates will conduct and document a mock food recall at least annually.

The exercise will test WPC's ability to:

  • identify a selected product;
  • locate supplier and receiving information;
  • identify inventory on hand;
  • determine whether the product entered production;
  • identify affected production dates;
  • identify client delivery locations;
  • identify appropriate recall contacts;
  • simulate required communications; and
  • document product disposition.

Management will review results and address deficiencies identified during the exercise.

Public Food Safety & Recall Notification

Wholesome Plates maintains the capability to publish public food-safety, food-hold, and recall notices through WholesomePlatesCatering.com when appropriate or required.

Public notices may identify the affected product, identifying information, relevant dates or locations when appropriate, instructions concerning the product, and company contact information.

Current notices are posted on our Food Safety & Recall Notices page.
