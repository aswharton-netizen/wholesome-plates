Food Safety & Compliance

BIOSECURITY & SUPPLY PROTECTION

Effective Date: August 2026

Purpose

Wholesome Plates maintains procedures designed to protect food, ingredients, supplies, production processes, storage areas, and deliveries from intentional contamination, tampering, theft, unauthorized access, or other threats to food safety.

These protections apply throughout purchasing, receiving, storage, preparation, production, packaging, staging, transportation, and delivery.

Shared Commercial Kitchen Controls

Wholesome Plates operates within a shared licensed commercial kitchen facility.

Because portions of the facility and equipment are accessible to other authorized food businesses, WPC maintains additional controls over food and supplies under its custody.

WPC refrigerated and frozen inventory is maintained within designated locked storage cages inside shared temperature-controlled storage areas. Dry inventory is maintained in designated secured storage.

Access to WPC-controlled inventory is limited to authorized personnel.

Food and ingredients are not left unsecured in common areas except as reasonably necessary during active receiving, production, staging, or transportation.

Evidence of unauthorized access, unexplained movement of inventory, compromised packaging, tampering, or another food-security concern will result in affected product being placed on hold pending management review.

Approved Suppliers & Receiving

Wholesome Plates purchases food and supplies from approved commercial suppliers.

Deliveries are received by a ServSafe-certified WPC employee or other authorized trained personnel.

At receiving, WPC evaluates products as appropriate for:

  • temperature;
  • condition;
  • packaging integrity;
  • contamination;
  • evidence of tampering; and
  • other indications that food safety may have been compromised.

When an approved supplier makes a key-drop delivery, WPC personnel inspect the products and verify applicable temperatures promptly upon arrival at the kitchen.

Products determined to be unsafe or unacceptable will be rejected or placed on hold pending management determination.

Production Area Security & Sanitation

WPC uses shared commercial kitchen equipment, including shared cooking and production equipment.

Before production, WPC personnel inspect applicable work areas and clean and sanitize food-contact surfaces and shared equipment before use.

WPC also cleans and sanitizes applicable equipment and production areas following use in accordance with company and facility procedures.

These controls supplement cleaning and facility-maintenance services provided by the shared commercial kitchen operator.

Employee Responsibilities

Employees are required to promptly report:

  • unauthorized individuals in restricted areas;
  • suspicious activity;
  • evidence of product tampering;
  • damaged or compromised packaging;
  • unexplained changes to WPC inventory;
  • suspected intentional contamination; or
  • other potential food-security concerns.

Employees shall not use food reasonably suspected of contamination or tampering.

Transportation & Delivery Security

Prepared food is wrapped, sealed, and placed into appropriate insulated catering carriers for transportation.

Food remains under WPC control during transportation and is delivered to designated client locations or authorized representatives.

Delivery documentation supports chain-of-custody and traceability requirements.

Biosecurity Incident Response

When intentional contamination, tampering, or another significant food-security threat is reasonably suspected, WPC will:

  1. stop use or distribution of affected product;
  2. isolate affected or potentially affected food;
  3. secure affected products or areas where appropriate;
  4. notify management;
  5. determine whether client locations may be affected;
  6. initiate recall/traceability procedures when warranted;
  7. notify affected clients as appropriate;
  8. contact appropriate regulatory or law-enforcement authorities when circumstances warrant;
  9. preserve relevant documentation; and
  10. document corrective action and final disposition.
